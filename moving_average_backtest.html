How the rule works

At each close, compare the total-return index with its average over the latest 200 sessions. If the index is above the average, hold it from the following close onward. Otherwise, switch to cash at that close.

Cash earns 0%. Each switch costs 5 basis points. Taxes and tracking differences are excluded. Both portfolios start at the end of the 200-session warm-up. Input history starts on 1988-01-04.
